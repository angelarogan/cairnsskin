Kleresca is a light-based treatment platform that works differently to
standard LED therapy, using what's known as fluorescent light energy to
target inflammation associated with rosacea.

## How it actually works

A specifically formulated gel is applied to the skin before the light
device is used. Compounds in the gel absorb the light and re-emit it as
fluorescent light energy, which penetrates the skin to stimulate its own
repair processes, rather than simply warming or resurfacing the surface
the way some other light-based treatments do. The session itself is
generally described as comfortable, involving a warm sensation rather than
any discomfort.

## What a course involves

Rosacea-focused treatment is delivered as a course of several sessions
rather than a single appointment, since the inflammation being targeted
builds down gradually rather than resolving instantly. Reported results
after completing a full course commonly last several months to around a
year before a top-up may be needed, though this varies between
individuals and rosacea itself can still flare from its usual triggers in
the meantime.

## What it can and can't do

This treatment is aimed specifically at the inflammation and visible
redness associated with rosacea, and is not a cure for the underlying
condition. Daily sun protection and avoiding personal triggers, heat,
alcohol, certain skincare actives, remain important alongside any
in-clinic treatment. A professional assessment beforehand matters, since
persistent facial redness can have causes other than rosacea that need a
different approach entirely.
