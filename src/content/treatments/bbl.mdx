Broadband light, usually shortened to BBL, is a light-based treatment
commonly used for sun-related pigmentation and redness. It works by
delivering broad-spectrum light that is absorbed by pigment and by small
blood vessels near the skin's surface, which can make sun spots, freckling
and some redness appear lighter or less visible over a course of treatments.

## What it's generally used for

BBL is most often discussed for concerns such as sun spots, freckling,
uneven skin tone and mild redness. It is a surface-level, appearance-focused
treatment rather than a way of reversing the cellular UV damage that caused
the pigmentation in the first place.

## What to expect

Most people need a course of several sessions rather than a single
treatment to see meaningful change, and improvement builds gradually.
Strict sun protection before and after treatment matters more than usual, in
a high-UV climate like Cairns, both to protect healing skin and to avoid
undoing the results.

## Suitability

Not everyone is a suitable candidate for BBL. Skin type, recent tan and
certain medical conditions can all affect suitability, which is why this is
a consultation-first treatment rather than one to book without an
individual skin assessment.
