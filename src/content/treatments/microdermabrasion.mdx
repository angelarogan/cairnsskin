Microdermabrasion is a mechanical exfoliation treatment that removes the
outermost layer of dead skin cells, aimed at smoothing texture and
refreshing dull-looking skin.

## What it's commonly used for

It is generally used for surface-level concerns such as rough texture,
dullness, mild congestion and enlarged-looking pores, since removing
built-up dead skin can make pores and texture appear refined immediately
after treatment. It is considered a lighter-intensity treatment compared to
options such as skin needling or fractional radiofrequency. See
[Microdermabrasion treatment information](https://www.laserclinics.com.au/skin-care-treatments/microdermabrasion/)
from Laser Clinics Australia for more on the specific protocol used.

## What it doesn't do

Microdermabrasion works at a surface level and does not address deeper
concerns such as established acne scarring, deep wrinkles or significant
pigmentation, which generally need different or more intensive approaches.
Results are also temporary, since skin naturally renews its surface layer
over time regardless of treatment.

## What to expect

Sessions are often done as part of an ongoing maintenance routine rather
than a one-off fix. Sun protection matters afterwards, since freshly
exfoliated skin can be more sensitive to UV in the days following
treatment. Treatment consultations are available at
