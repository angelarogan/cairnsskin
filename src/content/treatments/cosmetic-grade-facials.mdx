Cosmetic grade facials use professional-strength products and techniques,
typically beyond what's available in over-the-counter skincare, aimed at
refreshing and smoothing the skin's surface in a single session.

## What they're commonly used for

These facials are generally positioned as a broad, tailored treatment for
concerns such as dullness, congestion, dehydration and uneven texture,
combining cleansing, exfoliation and targeted serums or masks suited to the
skin being treated at the time. See
[Advanced Facials treatment information](https://www.laserclinics.com.au/skin-care-treatments/advanced-facials/)
from Laser Clinics Australia for more on the current facial menu.

## What they don't do

A single facial is not a substitute for a dedicated treatment plan for
persistent or specific concerns such as active acne, established
pigmentation or significant scarring, which generally respond better to
targeted treatments used consistently over time. Results from a facial are
also typically shorter-lived than more intensive in-clinic treatments.

## What to expect

Downtime is generally minimal, and many people build facials into a regular
skincare routine rather than a one-off treatment. As with any professional
skin treatment, the specific approach and suitability should be discussed at
consultation rather than assumed from a general description. Treatment
consultations are available at
[Laser Clinics Cairns Central](https://www.laserclinics.com.au/skin-care-clinic/cairns-central/).
