Pigmentation removal treatments are aimed at fading sun spots, freckling and
patchy discolouration, generally using light-based or laser technology to
break down excess melanin in the treated area.

## What it's commonly used for

This is generally discussed for sun-related pigmentation rather than
melasma, since the two respond differently to treatment. Getting an
accurate diagnosis of the type of pigmentation involved matters before
starting, since treating melasma with an approach suited to sun spots can
sometimes worsen it.

## What it doesn't do

Pigmentation removal treatment addresses existing, visible pigmentation. It
does not stop new pigmentation from forming if sun exposure continues
without adequate protection, and it is not a substitute for daily SPF.
Results also vary between individuals depending on skin type and the depth
of the pigmentation.

## What to expect

A course of sessions is often needed, and treated spots may darken
temporarily before fading. Because outcomes depend on accurately identifying
the type of pigmentation involved, a consultation is the appropriate next
step before starting treatment.
