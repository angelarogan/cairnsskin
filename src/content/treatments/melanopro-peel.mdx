Melanopro Peel is a two-phase cosmetic-grade treatment aimed at fading dark
spots and evening out tone, including some forms of melasma. Unlike a
single in-clinic peel, it deliberately combines a professional treatment
phase with a structured at-home routine.

## How the two phases work

The first phase happens in-clinic, where an intensive peeling masque is
applied for a set period before being removed later at home. The second
phase continues at home over the following weeks with prescribed
resurfacing and calming products, rather than the treatment being a single
one-off appointment. Because both phases matter, results depend on
completing the full routine as directed, not just attending the in-clinic
step.

## What it can help with

This approach is generally aimed at superficial to mixed-depth
pigmentation, dark spots and uneven texture, including sun-related
pigmentation and some presentations of melasma. It is not the right fit for
deeper, dermal melasma, which tends to respond poorly to peeling and
generally needs a different, more cautious approach, since the wrong active
treatment can make melasma worse rather than better. See
[Melanopro Peel treatment information](https://www.laserclinics.com.au/skin-care-treatments/melanopro/)
from Laser Clinics Australia for more on the specific protocol used.

## Before starting

Because this is an active peeling programme, skin generally needs to
already tolerate hydroxy acids or retinol-based products before starting.
A proper assessment of pigmentation type and depth matters here more than
usual, given how differently melasma can behave compared to general
sun-related pigmentation. Treatment consultations are available at
[Laser Clinics Cairns Central](https://www.laserclinics.com.au/skin-care-clinic/cairns-central/).
