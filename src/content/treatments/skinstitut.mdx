Skinstitut is a cosmeceutical skincare range used as a take-home option
alongside in-clinic treatments, including for pigmentation and uneven skin
tone. Cosmeceutical skincare generally means formulations with active
ingredients at higher concentrations than typical shop-bought products,
though it still sits below prescription-strength treatments.

## How it's generally used

For pigmentation specifically, this usually means a combination of daily
SPF, gentle exfoliation and brightening actives (such as vitamin C or
niacinamide-based formulations) selected to suit the individual's skin.
It's commonly recommended either as a starting point before considering
in-clinic treatment, or as a way of maintaining and supporting results
achieved through in-clinic pigmentation treatments such as BBL.

## What it can and can't do

Consistent use over weeks to months can help even out skin tone and
support results from other treatments. It is not a substitute for daily
sun protection, and it is not generally expected to meaningfully fade
deep or well-established pigmentation, or melasma, on its own. Cosmeceutical
skincare works best as part of a broader plan rather than as a stand-alone
fix for pigmentation that hasn't responded to basic skincare already.

## Suitability

Because the right combination of actives depends on skin type, sensitivity
and the specific type of pigmentation involved, product selection is best
done as part of a consultation rather than guessed at, particularly for
anyone with sensitive skin or a history of irritation from active
ingredients.
