Fractional radiofrequency (RF) combines radiofrequency energy with fine
needling or fractional delivery to stimulate collagen production deeper in
the skin, aimed at improving texture, firmness and the appearance of fine
lines or acne scarring.

## What it's commonly used for

It is generally discussed for skin texture, fine lines, mild to moderate
skin laxity and some types of acne scarring, working on the principle that
controlled heat and micro-injury encourage the skin to rebuild collagen over
time.

## What it doesn't do

Fractional RF is a non-surgical treatment, and it does not achieve the same
degree of change as surgical procedures for significant sagging or laxity.
It is not a one-off fix; meaningful improvement generally requires a course
of sessions, and results continue to develop gradually over the weeks and
months following treatment as new collagen forms.

## What to expect

Some redness and mild swelling for a short period afterwards is common.
Because suitability and expected outcomes depend heavily on individual skin
condition and the specific concern being treated, a consultation is the
appropriate way to set realistic expectations before starting a course.
