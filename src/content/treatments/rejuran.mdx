Rejuran is an injectable treatment based on polynucleotides, fragments of
DNA used to support the skin's own repair processes rather than to add
volume or directly resurface the skin.

## What it's commonly used for

Rejuran is typically injected in a series of sessions, aimed at improving
overall skin quality, including hydration, elasticity and fine texture. It
is generally positioned as a skin-quality treatment rather than an
anti-wrinkle or scar-removal treatment.

## What the evidence says

Polynucleotide treatments are a relatively newer category compared to
long-established options such as sunscreen or basic laser treatments, so
independent, large-scale research is still developing. Much of the
information available about specific outcomes and timelines comes from
manufacturer or brand material, which should be read as commercial
information rather than independent clinical evidence.

## Suitability and limitations

Rejuran is not a substitute for sun protection, and it is not generally
positioned as a treatment for deep acne scarring or significant volume
loss, where other options may be more appropriate. As with any injectable
treatment, individual suitability depends on skin condition, medical
history and expectations, which is why this is a consultation-first
treatment.
