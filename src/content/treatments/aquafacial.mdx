This style of hydrodermabrasion facial combines several steps, typically
cleansing, gentle exfoliation and infusion of hydrating serums, into a
single treatment aimed at refreshing the skin's appearance.

## What it's commonly used for

It's generally positioned as a gentler, more hydrating alternative to more
abrasive exfoliation treatments, and is often chosen for dull, congested or
dehydrated skin, or simply as a regular refresh between more intensive
treatments.

## What it doesn't do

As a single-session facial, it does not address deeper or more persistent
concerns such as active acne, established pigmentation or scarring, which
generally need a targeted treatment plan rather than a general facial.
Results are also short-term and typically need repeat sessions to maintain.

## What to expect

Because it's a gentler treatment, downtime is minimal for most people, and
many return to normal activities straight away. As with any professional
skin treatment, suitability still depends on individual skin condition,
which is best confirmed at consultation rather than assumed.
