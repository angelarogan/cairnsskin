Kleresca is a light-based treatment that works differently to standard LED
therapy, and it's one of the options sometimes used specifically for
rosacea-related redness and inflammation.

## How it actually works

A specifically formulated gel is applied to the skin before a light
device is used. Compounds in the gel absorb that light and re-emit it as
what's known as fluorescent light energy, which penetrates the skin to
stimulate its own repair processes, rather than simply warming or
resurfacing the surface the way some other light-based treatments do. The
session itself is generally described as comfortable, more of a warm
sensation than any discomfort.

## What a course actually involves

This isn't a single-session treatment. Rosacea-focused Kleresca is
delivered as a course of several sessions, since the inflammation being
targeted settles gradually rather than resolving instantly. Reported
results after completing a full course commonly last several months to
around a year, though this varies between individuals, and rosacea can
still flare again from its usual triggers in the meantime.

## What it can and can't do

Kleresca is aimed specifically at the visible redness and inflammation
associated with rosacea, not a cure for the underlying condition itself.
Daily sun protection and avoiding personal triggers, heat, alcohol, spicy
food, certain active skincare ingredients, remain important alongside any
in-clinic treatment, in Cairns' climate especially, where several common
rosacea triggers are present for most of the year.

## Before considering it

Persistent facial redness isn't always rosacea, other conditions can look
similar and need a different approach entirely, so a professional
assessment matters before starting any rosacea-specific treatment, to
confirm what's actually causing it.
