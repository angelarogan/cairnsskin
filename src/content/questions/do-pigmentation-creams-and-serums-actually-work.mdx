Pigmentation creams and serums are one of the most searched skincare
categories, and the honest answer is that some genuinely help, but
"pigmentation cream" covers an enormous range of formulations and
strengths, so the specific product matters more than the category.

## Why formulation and concentration matter

Two products can list the same active ingredient, vitamin C or
niacinamide, for example, and behave completely differently depending on
concentration, stability of the formula, and what else is in it. Cosmetic
or shop-bought products are not required to disclose concentration the
way cosmeceutical ranges typically do, which makes it hard to compare
products on ingredient lists alone. Cosmeceutical skincare, such as the
Skinstitut range, is generally formulated at higher, more consistent
active concentrations than typical shop-bought skincare, which is part of
why it's commonly recommended alongside in-clinic pigmentation
treatments rather than as a replacement for basic drugstore products.

## What a good cream or serum can do

Consistent use of a well-formulated product over weeks to months can help
even out skin tone, support results from in-clinic treatment, and slow how
quickly new pigmentation forms, particularly when paired with daily SPF.
This is a genuine, evidence-supported effect, not just marketing.

## What it generally can't do

Creams and serums are unlikely to meaningfully fade deep or long-established
pigmentation, or melasma, on their own, and none of them work as quickly as
in-clinic treatments such as BBL. Expecting a cream to replicate the speed
or strength of an in-clinic result is the most common source of
disappointment with this category.

## Getting the right one for your skin

Because concentration, formulation and your specific skin type all affect
whether a product actually helps, a consultation is a more reliable way to
find a suitable option than choosing based on packaging or marketing
claims alone, especially if you've tried shop-bought options without
seeing a difference.
