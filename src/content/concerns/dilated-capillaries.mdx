Dilated capillaries, sometimes called broken capillaries or telangiectasia,
are small blood vessels that have become permanently widened and visible
near the skin's surface, usually on the cheeks, nose or chin.

## Why they happen

Sun damage, extremes of temperature, genetics, rosacea and general skin
ageing can all weaken the walls of small blood vessels over time, causing
them to stay dilated rather than contracting back to their normal size.
Once a capillary has widened this way, it generally doesn't resolve on its
own.

## What treatment can do

Laser-based capillary reduction treatments target these vessels directly,
and can visibly reduce their appearance over a course of sessions. This is a
cosmetic improvement rather than a cure, since new capillaries can still
form over time, particularly with continued sun exposure or other
triggers. Daily sun protection helps limit new ones from developing.

## Suitability

Not everyone is a suitable candidate for capillary reduction treatment, and
suitability depends on skin type, the specific vessels involved and overall
skin condition. A consultation is the appropriate way to find out whether
treatment is likely to help in your specific case.
