Skin firmness depends on collagen and elastin, the structural proteins that
keep skin supported and able to spring back into place. As their production
naturally slows with age, and as cumulative sun damage breaks them down
further, skin can start to feel looser, particularly along the jawline,
cheeks and neck.

## Why it happens

Natural ageing reduces collagen and elastin production over time regardless
of lifestyle. UV exposure accelerates this significantly by damaging
existing collagen structures, which is why consistently high sun exposure
over many years, as is common in Far North Queensland, can contribute to
earlier or more pronounced loss of firmness than in a lower-UV climate.
Weight changes and genetics also play a role.

## What treatment can realistically do

Non-surgical treatments such as fractional radiofrequency are sometimes used
to stimulate collagen production and firm the skin's texture over a course
of sessions. These can produce a visible improvement in skin quality, but
they do not replicate the results of surgical procedures, and outcomes vary
considerably depending on the degree of laxity and individual skin
response.

## Setting expectations

Because the degree of sagging and what's achievable without surgery varies
so much between individuals, a consultation is the most reliable way to
understand realistic options for your specific skin, rather than relying on
before-and-after marketing images from other people's results.
