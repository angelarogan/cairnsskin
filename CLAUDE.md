# Cairns Skin — Project Instructions

Permanent, standing instructions for working in this repository. These apply
automatically in future sessions; they are not one-off task requests and
should be followed whenever treatment pages are created or updated.

## Laser Clinics Australia linking rule

For Cairns Skin treatment pages (`src/content/treatments/*.mdx`), where the
treatment is genuinely offered by Laser Clinics Australia, include exactly
two outbound links to Laser Clinics Australia:

1. **Official treatment page.** Link to the relevant official Laser Clinics
   Australia treatment page (for example, the Cairns Skin BBL page links to
   the official Laser Clinics Australia BBL page). Always find and verify
   the current, correct official URL before adding it. Never invent a URL.
   Use natural, non-keyword-stuffed anchor text such as "BBL treatment
   information" or "Skin Needling treatment information".

2. **Local Cairns link.** Link the words "Laser Clinics Cairns Central" to
   the official Laser Clinics Cairns Central location page:
   https://www.laserclinics.com.au/skin-care-clinic/cairns-central/. This
   should normally sit in the page's local/Cairns or consultation section,
   worded naturally, for example: "Treatment consultations are available at
   Laser Clinics Cairns Central."

**Structure:** Cairns Skin treatment page → official Laser Clinics Australia
treatment page, AND Cairns Skin treatment page → official Laser Clinics
Cairns Central page. The first establishes the relationship with the
specific treatment; the second establishes the relationship with the local
clinic.

**Don't overdo it.** One link to the official treatment page and one to
Cairns Central per treatment page is normally enough. Do not repeatedly link
to Laser Clinics throughout an article, and never use exact-match phrases
like "BBL Cairns" or "Skin Needling Cairns" purely as backlink anchor text.
Links must read naturally and be useful to the reader, not promotional.

**Question pages** (`src/content/questions/*.mdx`) should normally link
internally to the relevant Cairns Skin treatment hub rather than linking
directly to Laser Clinics Australia. The intended chain is: question page →
Cairns Skin treatment page → official Laser Clinics Australia treatment
page → Laser Clinics Cairns Central. This keeps Cairns Skin's internal
topical structure strong and avoids every article reading as promotional.
